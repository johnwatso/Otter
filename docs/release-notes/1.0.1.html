Otter 1.0.1

Version 1.0.1 · Build 2026102001 · Released 20 October 2026

A quick follow-up to Otter 1.0 that fixes two problems found on fresh installs of macOS Tahoe and makes opening a share over VPN feel finished.

🔔 Notifications during setup

  • Onboarding now asks macOS for permission to send notifications, so Connection Notifications is no longer shown as on when macOS was never asked.
  • The switch shows what macOS actually allows. If notifications were turned off for Otter, switching it on opens System Settings.

🏠 VPN shares reconnect at home

  • Fixes Manual and Connect Once shares with a VPN rule being unmounted again straight after connecting on your local network, so they would not reconnect after you came back from the VPN.
  • A mounted share is only let go when its server stops answering, not just because the VPN is down.

📂 Finder opens the share for you

  • When you open a share in Finder away from home and Otter connects the VPN and mounts it, Otter now opens the share in a Finder window, since Finder’s own attempt can only time out.
