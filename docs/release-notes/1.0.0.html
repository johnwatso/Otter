Otter 1.0

Version 1.0.0

Otter 1.0 keeps your SMB shares mounted: it reconnects after sleep, Wi-Fi changes and server hiccups, connects your VPN when a share needs it, and tells you when something is wrong.

Highlights since 0.5.0

  • Redesigned Manage Shares window with a reliability strip, per-share details and server overview.
  • Guided four-step onboarding that connects through the standard macOS sign-in.
  • Connect your VPN and mount shares automatically when a Finder SMB mount is attempted.
  • Per-share NAS diagnostics with a quick speed test, plus exportable diagnostic logs.
  • Custom share and server icons, and response-time and reliability history.
  • Tidier Settings: Wi-Fi and VPN status and backup actions are always visible, in a compact window.
  • Release hardening: privacy manifest, Swift 6 concurrency, and accessibility improvements.

Otter 1.0 supports SMB shares. NFS and WebDAV are not supported yet.
