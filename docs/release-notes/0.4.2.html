Otter 0.4.2

Version 0.4.2 · Build 2026083109 · Released 1 September 2026

Otter now prefers a server it can reach directly before reaching for a VPN, and the share controls say exactly what they do.

🏠 Direct connections first

No VPN when you are already at home.

  • In Manual, Adaptive and Connect Once modes, Otter prefers a locally reachable server before starting, or waiting for, a configured VPN.
  • Registered-network restrictions are still respected, and a VPN-only setup is treated as a fallback path.

🎛️ Clearer share controls

Manual shares and automatic shares behave the way their labels say.

  • Disconnecting a Manual share no longer creates an automatic-mount pause, and pause controls are hidden when there are no automatic shares.
  • Menu bar and Preferences wording now distinguishes Manual shares from shares Otter keeps connected.
  • New Always Show Server Name preference, plus singular and plural share counts in the menu.
