# Support

For setup questions and reproducible bugs, open an
[issue on GitHub](https://github.com/johnwatso/Otter/issues). Search existing
issues first and include:

- Otter version and build, macOS version, and whether the Mac is Apple silicon
  or Intel.
- Connection mode, and whether the
  problem follows sleep, a network change, or VPN use.
- What you expected, what happened, and steps that reproduce it.
- A diagnostic export, only after reviewing it.

Never post passwords, recovery keys, access tokens, or an unredacted network
share URL. Use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for a
security concern.

Before opening an issue, try **Test Setup** or **Diagnostics** for the share,
and make sure a direct Finder connection succeeds with the intended macOS
Keychain credential. The [NAS diagnostics guide](docs/nas-diagnostics.md)
explains what the diagnostic measurements do and do not prove.
